Hicham Eddoubi

PhD student in AI Security

I am a PhD student in the Italian National PhD Programme in Artificial Intelligence, run by Sapienza University of Rome in collaboration with the University of Cagliari. I work at the PRA Lab under the supervision of Prof. Battista Biggio.

My research asks a simple question: do the detectors we trust actually hold up when someone is trying to break them? I build benchmarks and attacks that stress-test detectors of AI-generated images, and I study how the same brittleness shows up in large language models and in malware classifiers under distribution drift.

Before the PhD I earned an MSc in Computer Engineering, Cybersecurity and Artificial Intelligence at the University of Cagliari (2024, with honours), and a BSc in Informatics and Mathematical Studies at Mohammed V University in Rabat.

Research interests

  • AI security
  • adversarial robustness
  • AI-generated image detection
