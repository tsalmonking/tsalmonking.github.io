Beyond Suffixes: Token Position in GCG Adversarial Attacks on Large Language Models

Hicham Eddoubi, Umar Faruk Abdullahi, Fadi Hassan

ICLR 2026 Workshop: I Can't Believe It's Not Better (ICBINB), 2026

GCG-style attacks conventionally append an adversarial suffix. We examine what happens when the optimised tokens are placed elsewhere in the prompt, and what that says about where a model's safety behaviour is actually anchored.

RAID: A Dataset for Testing the Adversarial Robustness of AI-Generated Image Detectors

Hicham Eddoubi, Jonas Ricker, Federico Cocchi, Lorenzo Baraldi, Angelo Sotgiu, Maura Pintor, Marcella Cornia, Lorenzo Baraldi, Asja Fischer, Rita Cucchiara, Battista Biggio

arXiv preprint arXiv:2506.03988, 2025

Detectors of AI-generated images report high accuracy, but they are almost always evaluated under idealised conditions. RAID is a dataset of 72k transferable adversarial examples, produced by an ensemble attack across seven detectors and four text-to-image generators, so that robustness can be measured without re-running an attack pipeline from scratch.
