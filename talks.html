04 NOV 2026 · MLSEC READING GROUP

Token positions in successful jailbreak attacks

Recent work on LLM jailbreaking aims to analyse the specific token positions affected by successful jailbreak attacks. In this talk, we will dive into the work presented in LLMs Encode Harmfulness and Refusal Separately (NeurIPS 2025), in which it is shown that harmfulness is encoded at the end of the instruction, separately from refusal at the chat-template tokens, and that some jailbreaks succeed by suppressing refusal while the recognition of harm survives. Subsequently, we take a look at Universal Jailbreak Suffixes Are Strong Attention Hijackers (TACL 2026), in which it is shown that adversarial suffixes work by hijacking attention at template tokens, and that stronger hijacking makes a suffix work on more prompts. These works highlight the inextricable nature of token positions, specifically the chat-template tokens, in the understanding and analysis of LLM safety.
